Reliable retail installation services for international brands and shopfitters

Opening a new commercial space or deploying a refreshed store concept abroad requires experienced hands on the ground who understand technical blueprints, local mall regulations, and tight delivery schedules. NAROMOUNT provides dedicated retail installation services and experienced crew support, acting as your local execution partner in Spain and Southern Europe without the overhead of flying your own teams across borders.

01 / STORE FIXTURES

Store fixture installation

Accurate assembly of wall systems, perimeter display standards, slotted uprights, graphic lightboxes, and commercial shelving. Engineered for high product density and flawless visual alignment.

  • Perimeter wall bays and slotted standards
  • Display gondolas and promotional endcaps
  • Secure mechanical wall anchoring and laser leveling
02 / MILLWORK & POS

Retail furniture installation

Expert assembly of bespoke joinery: cash wrap counters, glass showcases, central display tables, consultation desks, and fitting room dividers, with careful attention to fine hardware alignment and hidden wiring pass-throughs.

  • Cash desks, POS stations, and customer wraps
  • Tempered glass vitrines and locking pedestals
  • Concealed hinges, drawer runners, and fine trim
03 / PROGRAMMED ROLLOUTS

Retail rollout installation

Synchronized shopfitting installation for multi-location store chains. We implement scheduled updates, brand corner renovations, and overnight fixture swaps to keep your stores trading without downtime.

  • Overnight shifts in active shopping centers
  • Consistent quality across all rollout locations
  • Systematic packaging removal and site clearing

Common Queries

Frequently asked questions about retail installation

Do you install fixtures manufactured and delivered by overseas shopfitters?
Yes. We regularly act as the reliable on-the-ground installation partner for international millwork manufacturers and European shopfitters. We receive crated fixtures at the job site, unpack, inspect for transit integrity, and assemble according to your detailed CAD drawings and instructions.
Can your installation teams operate overnight or during mall non-trading hours?
Yes. Working night shifts, weekends, and designated mall closing hours is standard practice for our crews, subject to advance project planning, mall management permits, and crew availability. This ensures active shopping center locations and high-street boutiques experience minimal interruption to customer trading hours during rollouts and refurbishments.
What geographic areas do you cover from your Barcelona operational desk?
Based in Barcelona, we provide flexible operational deployment throughout Catalonia and across Spain (including Madrid, Valencia, and Bilbao), with established logistics capacity to handle scheduled projects in France, Italy, and other European destinations.
What languages do your team leads and project managers speak?
Our coordination desk and lead supervisors work fluently in English, Spanish, and Italian. We communicate directly with international project managers, general contractors, and local mall personnel, providing clear progress updates and photographic logs.
What is the procedure for booking an installation crew for an upcoming retail rollout?
Simply share your fixture manifest, architectural floor plans, target opening dates, and store location list. We assess the required crew size, confirm availability, and provide a clear quotation and operational deployment schedule.
